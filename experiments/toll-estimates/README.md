Contract: confirmed complete sum; clearly estimated complete sum; or insufficient/null with assessed/confirmed subtotals kept separate. Required paid-product inventory must be complete. A partial known subtotal is never a whole-route estimate; unknown is never zero. Coverage-key duplicates and family aggregate/part overlap reject composition rather than charging twice. Zero needs source-confirmed zero fare or independently confirmed no-paid-route.

Two estimate methods: retain an already source-bound numeric working quote as **estimated**, or take a **conservative upper bound** of a documented exhaustive set of comparable matrix alternatives for the same proven product domain. All alternatives must share operator/product/direction/category/payment, departure instant, route geometry, resolved fare validity and profile. No road substitution, arbitrary coefficient, averaging or highest national fare. Diagnostics retain min/max, sources and the explicit reason. The exhaustive scope reference is a trust-boundary input supplied by a verified adapter; a boolean alone is not independent proof. Missing such real evidence leaves the component unknown.

Unknown fare validity rejects estimation. Existing working/source-warning numeric quotes remain distinguishable from confirmed amounts and receipts. Prototype upper-bound policy is experimental, not activated business behavior.

The component's already resolved profile is part of its immutable comparison conditions and must equal every quote profile at the same departure instant.
