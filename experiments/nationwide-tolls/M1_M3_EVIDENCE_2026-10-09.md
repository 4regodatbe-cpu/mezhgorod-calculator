## Why OSM ID was initially null
`lib/toll-validator.ts` used Valhalla filter `node.osm_id`, while working M-4 tracer requests `edge.end_osm_node_id`. Initial M3 E2E showed 3 paid `toll_booth` events, all missing IDs. Fixed Valhalla filter and fallback `edge.end_node.node_id ?? edge.end_osm_node_id ?? edge.node_id`, then the real trace resolved exact M3 node IDs. This is a **verified causal debugging chain**.

## Source and validation limits
- OSM names verify booth identity, but not independently prove official tariff line completeness or how a full-corridor operator receipt composes rates. Valhalla complete booth event coverage is necessary but not always sufficient to infer every other operator charge. Customer total remains unknown.
- `auditM1M3SelectedRoute` returns `strictPriceAllowed:false` even on exact matched M3 booths.
- `quoteM3ThreeGateShadow` is **diagnostic only**, doesn't alter any actual price. A successful E2E is not authorization to change money.
- No verified physical OSM identification for M1 PVP 46 km yet. Approximate location near 46-km M1 map does not establish exact paid lane.
- Reverse date/time, unverified future calendar, different vehicle category, transponder payment and exceptional re-entry are not covered by this sample. No guess/prorating allowed.
